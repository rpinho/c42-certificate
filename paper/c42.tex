\begin{abstract}
We give an exact-input certificate for the upper bound
$C_{42}\le0.6888863712$ in the pure power-sum problem. The construction
refines the step profiles of Griego, R\"ohrig and Plata to 256 middle
blocks, without changing their coefficient-cancellation mechanism.
It improves the strongest public proposal located in our 22 October 2026
audit, $0.688970$, by $0.0000836288$. An unmodified public interval
checker and a separately written Arb checker verify the strict finite
inequalities. The asymptotic implication is proved below; no explicit
finite threshold, optimality, human peer review or formal verification
is claimed. All material remains local and unsubmitted.
\end{abstract}
\maketitle

\section{Statement and prior constructions}
Define
\[
 R_n=\min_{\substack{z_1,\ldots,z_n\in\CC\\\max_j|z_j|=1}}
       \max_{1\le k\le n}\left|\sum_{j=1}^n z_j^k\right|,
 \qquad C_{42}=\limsup_{n\to\infty}R_n.
\]
This is constant 42a in Tao and contributors' table~\cite{table}.
The condition is maximum modulus one; the other roots may lie inside
the unit disk.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
The pure power-sum constant satisfies
\[
 C_{42}\le\frac{215276991}{312500000}=0.6888863712.
\]
\end{theorem}

The table lists Harcos's $0.69368$ and Griego's proposed
$0.6906538$~\cite{biro,griego}. Open PR~184 proposes R\"ohrig's
eight-step bound $0.688983$~\cite{roehrig}. A separate public repository
by Kerrigan Plata proposes $0.688970$ using six nonuniform middle
levels~\cite{plata}. We reproduced both latter certificates before
optimizing. Plata's value is the strongest public upper proposal located,
and is the comparison used here; both proposals explicitly lack expert
peer review. The local audit is bounded, not an exhaustive priority claim.

We retain the generating function, free final block and Newton-identity
argument of these works. The changes are a finer middle profile and
new rational inputs. No new power-sum reduction theorem is needed.

\section{A finite-step criterion}
Put $\tau=1/3$ and $B=2/3$. Let
\[
 \tau=t_0<t_1<\cdots<t_M=B,\qquad
 \alpha=a+ib,\quad a>0,\quad s=1-\alpha,
\]
and choose middle values $\eta_0,\ldots,\eta_{M-1}$.
Set $\delta_0=\eta_0-s$ and
$\delta_j=\eta_j-\eta_{j-1}$ for $j>0$. Define
\[
 I_\alpha(x)=\int_0^x\frac{v^{\alpha-1}}{1-v}\,dv,
 \quad K=I_\alpha(\tau),\quad D=I_a(\tau)>0.
\]
For $t+r<1$ put
\[
 J_\alpha(t,r)=\int_0^{1-t-r}\frac{v^{\alpha-1}}{1-v}
       \log\frac{(1-t-v)(1-r-v)}{tr}\,dv,
\]
and put $J_\alpha(t,r)=0$ otherwise. Positive real powers use real
logarithms. This also equals the integral of
$(1-u-v)^{\alpha-1}/(uv)$ over $u\ge t$, $v\ge r$, $u+v\le1$.
Finally set
\begin{equation}\label{eq:Y}
 Y=1+sK-\sum_j\delta_j\bigl(I_\alpha(1-t_j)-K\bigr)
       +\frac12\sum_{i,j}\delta_i\delta_jJ_\alpha(t_i,t_j).
\end{equation}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:criterion}
If $|s|<C$, $|\eta_j|<C$ for all $j$, and $|Y|<CD$, then
$R_n\le C$ for every sufficiently large integer $n$. Consequently
$C_{42}\le C$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Put $A=\lfloor n/3\rfloor$. Split the indices into
$E=\{1,\ldots,A\}$, $M_n=\{A+1,\ldots,n-A-1\}$ and
$F=\{n-A,\ldots,n\}$. On $E$ set $S_m=s$. On $M_n$ put
\[
 w_m=\sum_j\delta_j\mathbf1_{m>\lfloor nt_j\rfloor},
 \qquad S_m=s+w_m.
\]
Every such value is one of the $\eta_j$. Write
$\beta_l=(\alpha)_l/l!$ and $P_m=n\beta_{n-m}/m$ on $F$.
These numbers are nonzero because $a>0$. Set
\begin{align*}
 Y_n={}&n\beta_n-n\sum_{m\in M_n}\frac{w_m\beta_{n-m}}m
 +\frac n2\sum_{\substack{u,v\in M_n\\u+v\le n}}
       \frac{w_uw_v\beta_{n-u-v}}{uv}+s\sum_{m\in F}P_m,\\
 S_m={}&\frac{Y_n}{\sum_{r\in F}|P_r|}
              \frac{\overline{P_m}}{|P_m|}\qquad(m\in F).
\end{align*}
Consider coefficients $b_l$ defined by
\[
 \mathcal B(z)=\frac1{1-z}
     \exp\left(-\sum_{m=1}^n\frac{S_mz^m}{m}\right)
       =\sum_{l\ge0}b_lz^l.
\]
Relative to $(1-z)^{-\alpha}$ every correction index exceeds $A$.
Since $3(A+1)>n$, at most two corrections contribute to $b_n$.
A final correction plus any other correction has index at least $n+1$.
Expanding the exponential therefore gives exactly
$nb_n=Y_n-\sum_{m\in F}S_mP_m=0$.

Let $y_2,\ldots,y_n$ be the roots of
$Z^{n-1}+b_1Z^{n-2}+\cdots+b_{n-1}$, with multiplicity, and let $y_1=1$.
The logarithmic derivative of $\mathcal B$ and Newton's identities
give the same triangular recurrence for $S_k$ and
$Q_k=\sum_j y_j^k$ through $k=n$; at the last step use $b_n=0$.
Hence $Q_k=S_k$ for $1\le k\le n$. Dividing all roots by
$\Lambda=\max_j|y_j|\ge1$ cannot increase these power-sum moduli.

It remains to bound the final powers. The gamma-ratio asymptotic is
\[
 \beta_l=\frac{l^{\alpha-1}}{\Gamma(\alpha)}(1+O(l^{-1})),
 \qquad \sum_{l\le L}|\beta_l|=O((L+1)^a).
\]
Away from $l=0$, ordinary Riemann sums give
\[
 \Gamma(\alpha)n^{-\alpha}Y_n\longrightarrow Y,
 \qquad |\Gamma(\alpha)|n^{-a}\sum_{m\in F}|P_m|
          \longrightarrow D.
\]
For completeness, discard first $l<\varepsilon n$ in each sum.
In a single sum $m\asymp n$. In a double sum group by
$l=n-u-v$: there are at most $n$ pairs and $uv\gg n^2$.
After the relevant normalization the discarded contribution is
$O(\varepsilon^a+n^{-a})$ in both cases. This permits
$\varepsilon\downarrow0$, including the singular endpoint.
The finite number of step discontinuities and the integer floors do
not affect the limit. Thus
$|Y_n|/\sum_F|P_m|\to|Y|/D<C$. All final powers have modulus below
$C$ eventually, which proves the assertion for all sufficiently large
$n$, not merely a subsequence.
\end{proof}

\section{Rational certificate and arithmetic}
Use $M=256$,
\begin{align*}
 t_j&=\frac{256+j}{768}\quad(0\le j\le256),\qquad C=0.6888863712,\\
 \alpha&=0.64427390171288+0.58993506009068i.
\end{align*}
The 256 pairs $\eta_j$ are the terminating-decimal rational strings in
\texttt{certificates/c42\_256.json}. The complete certificate, including
these data, has SHA-256
\begin{center}\small\ttfamily
705a729155eb508878d17ebea0322627\\
a0ae02451c044a346a99d2e81c7afcab.
\end{center}
The file is the explicit definition of the profile; unverified floating
search diagnostics retained in it are not inputs to the proof.
The exact minimum squared profile margin is
\[
 \min\{C^2-|s|^2,C^2-|\eta_j|^2\}
 =\frac{1491623179869746287}{5000000000000000000000000000}>0.
\]
Outward enclosures, shortened here, give
\begin{align*}
 D&\in[0.892184209107241336566750826518,
          0.892184209107241336566750826519],\\
 |Y|/D&\in[0.688886370983465645822767803129,
             0.688886370983465645822767803130],\\
 C^2D^2-|Y|^2&\in[2.37472575330897264258,
                      2.37472575330897264259]\,10^{-10}.
\end{align*}
Proposition~\ref{prop:criterion} proves Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.

Two independently authored implementations accept these inputs. The first
is R\"ohrig's unchanged public \texttt{mpmath.iv} checker at 100 decimal
places, using explicit geometric series tails for $I$ and $J$.
The second, written by an independent agent from the mathematical notes
without reading either search code or the first checker, uses 768-bit
Arb balls. It computes $I$ through
\[
 I_\alpha(x)=\frac{x^\alpha}{\alpha}
 {}_2F_1(1,\alpha;\alpha+1;x)
\]
and $J$ through a separately bounded logarithmic series with 400 terms.
No optimization status, sampled integral or floating-point maximum is
accepted as a certificate. Corrupted profiles, malformed thresholds and
the too-strong radius $0.6888863709$ are rejected.

\section{Reproduction and limits}
The local package includes pinned public source code, both checkers,
exact data and transcripts. From its root, with Python, mpmath and
python-flint available, run
\begin{verbatim}
python verify_primary.py certificates/c42_256.json
python independent/check_bounds.py \
  certificates/c42_256.json --bits 768 --terms 400
\end{verbatim}
The proof gives existence of an eventual threshold, not its numerical
value. The optimizer's final status was a line-search warning with tiny
floating constraint residuals; only the subsequent strict interval
checks justify the result. Neither the profile nor its resolution is
claimed optimal. The independent review is a separate AI-agent review,
not independent human expert review or proof-assistant formalization.
Final source checks and the exact search commands are recorded in the
local \texttt{PRIOR\_ART.md} and \texttt{REPORT.md}.
